More than a coffee shop

A place to pause.

The Matcha House began with a simple belief: the best part of coffee and matcha is what they make possible.

Our company

Built around the daily ritual.

We opened our doors in 2019 with a tiny espresso machine, a borrowed record player, and a lot of optimism. Today, we are still independently owned and committed to making the neighborhood feel a little more connected.

Our baristas know your order, our tables welcome your workday, and our beans are roasted to make every morning taste considered.

Our mission

Make room for good things.

We create excellent coffee and an open-hearted space where people can slow down, meet up, and feel known.

Our vision

A stronger neighborhood, one cup at a time.

We imagine a future where local coffee shops are gathering places for creativity, care, and lasting community.

Meet the owner

“Coffee is the invitation.”

Ivan Martinez founded The Matcha House after a decade of hospitality work and an unreasonable number of notebook sketches.

She believes a café should be both beautifully ordinary and quietly meaningful: a place where the regulars feel at home and the first-timers know exactly where to sit.

Ivan Martinez, founder & Matcha obsessive
